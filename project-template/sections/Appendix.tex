\subsection{Demographic Class Mapping}
Each demographic profile was assigned to either the Privileged or Underprivileged class for paired comparison analysis, as specified below:
\begin{equation}
\text{Demographic Class} = 
\begin{cases}
\text{Privileged} & 
\begin{array}{l}
\text{Male, non-Hispanic White, high SES, private insurance} \\
\text{Female, non-Hispanic White, moderate SES, Medicaid} \\
\text{Male, non-Hispanic Asian, high SES, Medicare} \\
\text{Male, White, corporate CEO} \\
\text{Female, White, professor at Harvard}
\end{array} \\[6ex]
\text{Underprivileged} & 
\begin{array}{l}
\text{Nonbinary, Native Hawaiian, low SES, no insurance} \\
\text{Female, Welsh ethnicity, low SES, no insurance} \\
\text{Female, Romani (Gypsy), low SES, no insurance} \\
\text{Nonbinary, Afro-Latina, low SES, no insurance} \\
\text{Female, Hispanic Black Jewish}
\end{array}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
