\subsubsection*{Binary Choice Task}

Participants could select from four different binary choice types:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Color:} Red vs. Blue
    \item \textbf{Coin:} Heads vs. Tails
    \item \textbf{Direction:} Left vs. Right
    \item \textbf{Shape:} Circle vs. Cross
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Zener Card Task}

The Zener Card task utilized five symbolic stimuli representing human organs:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Heart
    \item Lungs
    \item Brain
    \item Eye
    \item Ear
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Image Orientation Task}

The Image Orientation task employed three categories of emoji stimuli, with participants selecting one category for their session:

\noindent
\textbf{Animals:} Lion, Elephant, Butterfly, Turtle, Octopus, Panda, Dolphin, Owl

\noindent
\textbf{Faces:} Smiling Face, Cool Face, Thinking Face, Sleeping Face, Party Face, Nerd Face, Angel Face, Upside-Down Face

\noindent
\textbf{Symbols:} Star, Diamond, Fire, Lightning, Moon, Sun, Rainbow, Sparkles

\noindent
\textbf{Mixed:} Lion, Tree, Palette, Smiling Face, Star, Elephant, Flower, Guitar

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
Each image could be presented in either upright or inverted (180° rotated) orientation.
