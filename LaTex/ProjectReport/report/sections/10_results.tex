\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

All numbers are taken from the captured bench run on the
production ward. Six separate experiments, one summary table each,
one or two takeaways per experiment.

\subsection{Result 1 --- FCFS vs.\ Priority (legacy bench)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l r r r}
\toprule
\textbf{Urgent patients (CRIT+HIGH)} & \textbf{FCFS} & \textbf{Priority} & \textbf{$\Delta$} \\
\midrule
First task starts at (min)   &   134 &   155 & $+21$ \\
All tasks done by (min)      & 8{,}481 & 3{,}104 & $\bm{-5{,}377}$ \\
Total overtime (min)         & 20{,}210 & 20{,}210 & 0 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Priority starts urgent care 21 minutes later but finishes
        urgent patients $\sim$5{,}378~minutes sooner on average.
  \item Same total work; same overtime. Priority just changes
        \emph{who waits how long}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Result 2 --- Ordering vs.\ matching (2$\times$2 bench)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l l r r r}
\toprule
\textbf{Cell} & \textbf{Combination} & \textbf{Makespan} &
\textbf{Breaches} & \textbf{Runtime} \\
\midrule
A & FCFS $\times$ Greedy        & 9{,}260 & 1{,}827 & 12.0~ms \\
B & FCFS $\times$ Hungarian     & 9{,}350 & 1{,}830 & 65.5~ms \\
C & Priority $\times$ Greedy    & \textbf{9{,}260} & \textbf{1{,}820} & 15.9~ms \\
D & Priority $\times$ Hungarian & 9{,}470 & 1{,}819 & 84.2~ms \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Hungarian is $\sim$4$\times$ slower and \emph{adds}
        $90$--$210$ minutes of makespan.
  \item Cell C (Priority $+$ Greedy) wins on every column.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Result 3 --- Best lexicographic priority policy}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l r l}
\toprule
\textbf{Policy} & \textbf{Urgent finish (min)} & \textbf{vs.\ default} \\
\midrule
\code{acuity\_young\_first}      & \textbf{2{,}696} & $-13\%$ (best) \\
\code{acuity\_old\_first}        & 2{,}714 & $-12\%$ \\
\code{acuity\_first} (default)   & 3{,}104 & --- \\
\code{priority\_first}           & 3{,}395 & $+9\%$ \\
\code{acuity\_short\_first}      & 3{,}562 & $+15\%$ \\
\code{acuity\_long\_first}       & 3{,}604 & $+16\%$ \\
\code{duration\_first}           & 7{,}910 & $+155\%$ (worst) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Same input, same matcher --- only the tie-break rule
        differs.
  \item Best vs.\ worst is a $2.9\times$ swing on the urgent-care
        metric.
  \item Free $13\%$ improvement just by switching the default.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Result 4 --- Fairness sweep}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l r r r}
\toprule
\textbf{Matcher} & \textbf{Throughput} & \textbf{Within-role spread}
                 & \textbf{Runtime} \\
\midrule
Greedy             & \textbf{0.218} & \textbf{7.1}  & 15.9~ms \\
Hungarian          & 0.213          & 73.5          & 84.5~ms \\
Fair($\alpha=0.75$) & 0.218         & 12.6          & 15.7~ms \\
Fair($\alpha=0.50$) & 0.218         & 21.8          & 15.7~ms \\
Fair($\alpha=0.25$) & 0.218         & 32.0          & 15.7~ms \\
Fair($\alpha=0.00$) & 0.218         & 42.0          & 15.7~ms \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Greedy is already the fairest matcher on this ward.
  \item Workers all start at zero load $\Rightarrow$ ``fair-mode''
        rebalancing has nothing to redistribute.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Result 6 --- Adversarial wards}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Four hand-built pathological wards;
        every strategy produced an identical schedule on every
        ward.
  \item Hungarian still cost $2$--$4\times$ Greedy's wall-clock for
        no schedule improvement.
  \item Per-scenario tables in Appx.~\ref{app:supplementary}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Recommended configuration}

\begin{insightbox}
\textbf{Best on this dataset:}\;\code{Priority} ordering $+$
\code{Greedy} matching $+$ \code{acuity\_young\_first} priority
policy.\\[0.2em]
Wins on every axis the clinician cares about, at the lowest
runtime.
\end{insightbox}
