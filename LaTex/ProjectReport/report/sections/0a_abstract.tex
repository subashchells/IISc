\begin{center}
  {\Large\scshape Abstract}\\[0.3em]
  \rule{0.25\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.8em}

\noindent
\textbf{CardioScheduler} is a C++ task-allocation engine for
cardiology hospital wards that decouples \emph{which task to consider next}
(ordering) from \emph{which worker takes it} (matching) into pluggable
strategy interfaces.

\vspace{0.9em}
\noindent\textbf{What was built.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Discrete-event scheduler with two pluggable policy axes
        (ordering, matching).
  \item Three matchers: \code{Greedy}, \code{FairGreedy($\alpha$)},
        \code{Hungarian}.
  \item Seven-policy lexicographic priority catalogue subsuming the
        legacy scalar ordering.
  \item Multi-objective Pareto frontier with NSGA-II crowding
        selection on (urgent-care, makespan, max overtime).
  \item Six-mode bench suite (legacy, 2$\times$2, Pareto, fairness,
        adversarial, urgency), self-contained ward-editor web UI.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.9em}
\noindent\textbf{Production-ward sample dataset.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 100 patients and 10 staff members on the sample ward.
  \item Drawn from a catalogue of 418 diseases, 183 tests, and 325
        inter-test dependency edges.
  \item Produces 2{,}017 per-patient task instances at runtime.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.9em}
\noindent\textbf{Three headline findings.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ordering moves more than matching.}
        Switching the lex-policy tie-break from \code{acuity\_first}
        to \code{acuity\_young\_first} cuts the mean last-finish of
        urgent (CRITICAL+HIGH) patients from 3{,}104 to 2{,}696
        minutes ($-13\%$). No scalar weighted-sum priority can
        recover this.
  \item \textbf{Hungarian regresses CRIT timing.}
        Although globally optimal per tick, Hungarian increases
        mean CRITICAL last-finish by $+85\%$ and $p_{90}$ by
        $+311\%$ vs.\ Greedy, at $\sim\!3.6\times$ wall-clock.
        Mechanism: one-task-per-worker-per-tick deferrals on
        identical priority keys.
  \item \textbf{No scheduling change creates capacity.}
        20{,}210~min of overtime are invariant across every
        strategy --- the ward's tasks are assigned by guideline
        rather than by real-world work allocation.
\end{itemize}
